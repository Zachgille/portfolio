RECONSTRUCTION PIPELINE

Two sources of information.
One reconstructed picture.

01 / Map

Recover the virtual stream layout.

Work began from a decrypted Blu-ray ISO of Panasonic VWBS-1531. Parsing EDL.bin and its virtual mapping was part of reconstructing the base and enhancement streams presented by the disc format.

02 / Inspect

Read beyond the base picture.

The investigation examined virtual MVC/H.264 streams and MGVC SEI/data structures. The AVC base provides only part of the information required for the intended reconstruction.

03 / Reconstruct

Account for the lossy base encode.

Panasonic patent US9392246B2 informed the residual reconstruction process, including compensation for errors introduced by the lossy base encode. The goal is to reconstruct encoded sample information, not fabricate additional image detail.

04 / Validate

Check samples before packaging.

The work includes validation of reconstructed samples. The intended delivery format is 1920 × 1080, 23.976 fps Main12 HEVC in MKV, preserving original SDR colorimetry, audio, subtitles, and chapters. End-to-end output completion is not claimed here.

CONTEXT

Make an unfamiliar
format legible.

The source was Panasonic’s Japanese Blu-ray of The Wind Rises, release VWBS-1531. Its proprietary MGVC video combines AVC base video with enhancement data. The project explored how those pieces fit together and how to reconstruct 12-bit 4:2:0 samples.

The hard part was not selecting an output codec. It was understanding virtual mapping, stream structure, and the meaning of enhancement information well enough to reconstruct the intended video.

TECHNICAL APPROACH

Follow the structure,
then check the samples.

I worked with EDL.bin, virtual MVC/H.264 base and enhancement streams, and MGVC SEI/data structures. Panasonic patent US9392246B2 supplied context for the residual reconstruction process and compensation for lossy base-encode errors.

That separates reconstruction from increasing bit depth by simple conversion. The objective is to use information carried in the source to recover samples, rather than upscale an image or invent detail.

DELIVERY TARGET

A conventional container,
with the source’s characteristics.

  • Main12 HEVC video in an MKV container
  • 1920 × 1080 at 23.976 fps
  • Original SDR and colorimetry preserved
  • Disc audio, subtitles, and chapters retained

These are the intended output requirements. The project account supports reconstruction work and sample validation, but does not establish a completed, fully validated end-to-end encode.

WHAT THIS WORK SHOWS

Debugging without
a familiar abstraction.

This project required moving between binary structures, virtual stream layouts, patent descriptions, and decoded samples. It demonstrates how I investigate unfamiliar systems and distinguish a plausible interpretation from an output that has actually been validated.
