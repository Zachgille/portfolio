Selected work

Lumaire

Android TV client · Kotlin, Jetpack Compose, Room

A media client built for a television and a directional remote. The Movies and Series screens open with sixteen genre shelves, and each shelf used to run its own query, repeating the same work. Now they share one candidate set inside SQLite.

Case studySource

TriageFlow

Multi-tenant support desk · TypeScript, NestJS, PostgreSQL

Tickets, replies, internal notes and SLA checks for many companies sharing one database. Tenant isolation is enforced in the backend and the schema, and tested against a live database. With 50,000 seeded tickets, one composite index turned the main queue’s sequential scan into an index scan.

Case studySource

MGVC decoder

Reverse engineering · Python, C, H.264/MVC

Panasonic’s MGVC Blu-rays carry an ordinary 8-bit picture plus an enhancement stream that only Panasonic’s own players use. I worked out how the two combine, from the stream layout and Panasonic’s patent, and wrote tools that rebuild the 12-bit frames.

Case studySource

Bracket optimizer

March Madness pools · Python, DuckDB, scikit-learn

The likeliest bracket often isn’t the one most likely to win your pool, because plenty of other people pick it too. This pipeline models matchups, simulates the tournament 50,000 times, and scores candidate brackets against simulated opponents built from public pick rates.
